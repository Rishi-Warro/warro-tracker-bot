# Warro Tracker Bot

**Warro Tracker Bot** is a Telegram-based OSINT and digital-investigation support platform developed exclusively for verified **Law Enforcement Agency (LEA)** personnel conducting legally authorized investigations.

> **Important:** The source code, deployable templates, infrastructure configuration, and sensitive operational logic are not publicly available. This repository is maintained as a professional project showcase.

---

## 🎭 Main Feature: Operator-Controlled Undercover Engagement

What if an authorized investigator could communicate with an investigative subject through a controlled male or female decoy persona—without exposing the investigator’s real identity?

This is the main capability of **Warro Tracker Bot**.

The bot includes an **Operator-Controlled Undercover Engagement Module** that enables an authorized investigator to:

* Create a case-appropriate fictional male or female persona
* Communicate with an investigative subject through the Telegram bot
* Maintain separation between the investigator’s real identity and operational persona
* Conduct controlled engagement during authorized undercover operations
* Maintain relevant interaction records for investigation and evidence handling

This capability can support investigations involving:

* Cyber fraud
* Romance scams
* Escort-service scams
* Online extortion
* Impersonation
* Online exploitation
* Organized digital-crime networks

The module is completely operator-controlled. The authorized investigator remains responsible for every message, decision, and investigative action.

## 🧠 What the Bot Does

The platform can support the following high-level investigation workflow:

1. A verified LEA operator accesses the bot.
2. The operator creates an authorized case session.
3. The operator selects an appropriate investigative theme.
4. The bot generates a case-specific investigative link.
5. Available technical indicators are recorded when the link is opened.
6. GPS or camera information is collected only when the required browser permission is granted.
7. The investigator may communicate through an approved fictional persona.
8. Relevant information can be preserved for authorized case analysis.

The information collected by the platform should be treated as an investigative lead and independently corroborated through lawful procedures.
